About me

I'm a second-year CS student at Georgia Tech on a three-year track, graduating in Spring 2028. My threads are Intelligence and Information Internetworks, which is a formal way of saying I like models and I like the systems that have to serve them.

Most of my work so far has been applied machine learning. I do undergraduate research in Georgia Tech's Sustainable Urban Systems Lab on urban microclimate forecasting, using temporal fusion transformers behind a FastAPI service. Before that I interned as a software engineer building a React and Django inventory system, demand forecasting with XGBoost, and a voice agent on Azure.

What I'm actually after is the overlap: I want to be the engineer who can build the thing and also explain to the people paying for it why it's worth building. CS 2340 is the web half of that education.
